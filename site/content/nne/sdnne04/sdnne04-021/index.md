  Construction, tradition and modernism permeate the action of the architect José Liberal de Castro in his professional activity. One thing feeds the other, activating an unquestionable integrity. Modern convinced, is, above all, a profound expert of construction techniques of the past and present. Without falling into pastiche, the architect, when the project requires, appeals to time-honored architectural practices and removes from the past typological references adapting it to the modernist assumptions.
