abstract_en: |
  This work aims to discuss some of the issues that involve the production of the history of modern architecture in the current context, identifying ruptures and continuities in the historiography of recent decades in relation to the canonical construction of modernism. In this sense, the text rescues some aspects of 20th century historiography and addresses the construction of modernism as a movement, to discuss the permanence and use of the idea of “schools” of architecture in Brazilian historiography and the worldwide appreciation of the collections of the great names of the country's modern architecture.
