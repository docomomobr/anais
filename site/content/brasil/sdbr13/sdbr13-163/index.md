abstract_en: |
  In 1962 Le Corbusier travelled to Brazil for the third and last time. He had been invited by the French government to carry out the design of French Embassy at recently inaugurated Brasilia. This paper presents a synthesis of the facts before and after that commission, which was first offered to French- born, Brazilian established architect Jacques Pilon; and after Le Corbusier’s demise in August 1965 it was finally carried on by Chilean architect Guillermo Jullian de la Fuente, one of the last collaborators of the master, to whom it was requested a new project in 1970, elaborated and constructed between 1972-1974. This research confirms some of the information published in the previous research, complements it with more details and undoes some mistakes.
