abstract_en: |
  The main purpose of this essay is to promote discussion of an aspect of the (Brazilian) modern architecture, which is totally neglected. As Edwin Heathcote has written, it is symptomatic of the contemporary inability to confront death that the subject of funerary architecture has been largely avoided. However, as a starting point as well as a conscious methodological approach, the text is structured on shortcuts and/ or close readings of tombs designed by (Brazilian) modern architects like Rino Levi and Lina Bo Bardi, with an addendum on the “funerary planning” ideology.
