abstract_en: |
  This work investigates banking architecture in João Pessoa, starting from a perspective of expansion of modern architecture, concomitant with the urban expansion of the city. The bibliographic review on the subject, studies on urban transformations and the cataloging and mapping of institutions built between 1930 and 1980, led to the selection of four projects for analysis, two branches of Banco do Brasil and two of Banco do Estado da Paraíba. The study reveals changes in the typology: in the 1930s-40s, with branches built in the central nucleus, including a certain standardization and a modular structure; in the 1950s and 60s, the reception of Brazilian modern architecture; and in the 1970s-80s, mixed use and glazed facades, incorporating new exterior/interior relationships in areas of city expansion. It is expected to contribute to the discussion on the subject, as well as on the construction of the image of modernity in the capital of Paraíba.
