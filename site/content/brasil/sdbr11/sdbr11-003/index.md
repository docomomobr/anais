Dois personagens apresentam grande importância neste cenário de mudanças, são eles a arquiteta ítalo-brasileira Lina Bo Bardi e o economista Celso Furtado. abstract_en: |
  In the early 1960s, the Brazilian Northeast underwent sweeping changes in the fields of economy, politics and culture. Two characters have great importance to this scenario of changes, they are the Italian-Brazilian architect Lina Bo Bardi and the economist Celso Furtado. Furtado served as superintendent of SUDENE, whose goals were to improve population's quality of life, increase income and industrialize the Northeast. The architect worked in the cultural field restoring the architectural ensemble of the Solar do Unhão, directing the Museum of Modern Art of Bahia and proposing a School of Industrial Design and Crafts, which would be instaled at the museum. The school project have been interrupted by the military coup of 1964, but its design shows that this would be the result of Lina and Furtado’s political and economic thought for industrial development in the Northeast at the time.
